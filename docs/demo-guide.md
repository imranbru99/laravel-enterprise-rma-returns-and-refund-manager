### 2. 1-Click Interactive Step Progression
On `/demo`:
1. Observe the live RMA state machine diagram.
2. Click **Advance: Auto-Approve** to authorize the return.
3. Click **Advance: Receive at Hub** to simulate courier delivery.
4. Click **Advance: Inspection Grade A** to complete condition checklist.
5. Click **Advance: Issue Refund** to trigger gateway payout and financial journal posting.
6. Real-time audit logs and financial balances update dynamically on the page!
